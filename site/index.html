Everything you need to learn from real videos

Two subtitles at once

Read each line in your new language, with your own language below it. Replay a line, pause after each line, or hide the translation to test yourself.

Understand any word

Click a word to see what it means in this sentence, with example sentences and the sound of the word.

See what you already know

Set your level once. Words you know turn green, and the words to learn next turn orange.

Remember what you learn

Save words and lines with one click. Flashcards come back just before you would forget them. Export to Anki or CSV.

Find something to watch

Browse new videos from hand-picked channels in 18 languages, and see which videos have subtitles made by people.

The AI you choose

Use your own key for Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok or OpenRouter, or run a free model on your own computer.

Sync and backup

Sync your words, review progress and settings through your own Google Drive, or keep a backup file.

19 languages

Arabic, Chinese, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Hebrew, Hindi, Italian, Korean, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish and Ukrainian.

A closer look

Your data stays yours

LingoMingo has no server. Your words and settings stay in your browser, and the text you translate goes only to the AI service you choose. The Google Drive sync uses a hidden folder in your own Drive and cannot see your other files.
