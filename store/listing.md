### Description

Copy the text between the lines. The store shows plain text (no Markdown), and it keeps the line breaks.

---

Learn a language with the YouTube videos that you like. 📚

LingoMingo shows two subtitles at the same time: one in the language that you learn, and one in your own language. Click a word to see what it means. Save it, and review it later.

🎬 DUAL SUBTITLES ON YOUTUBE
• See the subtitles in your study language and in your own language together.
• Go to the previous line, repeat the line, or go to the next line with one click, or with the keys A, S and D.
• Auto-pause stops the video after each line, so you have time to read it.
• The subtitle list next to the video shows all lines. Click a line to go to it.
• Show or hide the subtitles with Shift+C, and the subtitle list with Shift+S.

💬 WORD POPUP
• Click a word in the subtitles to see its meaning in that sentence.
• Mark the word as Known, Learn or Skip.

🎨 VOCABULARY COLORS
• Set your vocabulary level one time. LingoMingo then shows the words that you know in green, the words to learn next in orange, and rare words in a darker color.

🧠 SAVE AND REVIEW
• Save words and full subtitle lines (phrases). Each word keeps its sentence and its video.
• Review your words with flashcards. The FSRS spaced-repetition scheduler shows each word again at the right time.
• Export your saved items as a CSV or JSON file, for example to use them in other flashcard apps.

📺 FIND VIDEOS
• The Media page shows the newest videos of YouTube channels in 18 study languages.
• Badges show which videos have human-made subtitles.
• Pin your favorite channels to the top of the list.

🌍 LANGUAGES
Arabic, Chinese, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Hebrew, Hindi, Italian, Korean, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish and Ukrainian. Right-to-left languages show correctly.

🤖 AI TRANSLATION
The word popup uses AI. When a video has no human-made subtitles in your language, AI also translates the subtitles. Add your own API key from a supported AI provider, or use a local AI model on your computer without a key. The Settings page shows the list of supported providers. Your AI provider can charge you for its use.

🔒 PRIVACY
• No account, no server, no analytics, no ads.
• Your settings, saved words and API keys stay in your browser.
• Subtitle lines and the words that you open go only to the AI provider that you select.

❤️ FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
LingoMingo is free and open source (GNU GPL v3).
Source code: https://github.com/mort3za/lingomingo

LingoMingo is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
