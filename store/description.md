Learn a language from the YouTube videos you already love. 📚

Watch with two subtitles at once: the language you are learning, and your own. Click any word to understand it, save it, and review it later. Real videos, real language, at your own pace.

🎬 WATCH WITH TWO SUBTITLES
• Read every line in your new language, with your own language right below it.
• Replay a line as many times as you need, or let the video pause after each line so you have time to read.
• Follow the full transcript next to the video, and jump to any line with one click.
• Ready for a challenge? Hide the translation and test yourself.
• Click, or keep your hands on the keyboard: every main action has a shortcut.

💬 UNDERSTAND ANY WORD
• Click a word to see what it means in this sentence, not just a long dictionary list.
• See example sentences that show how people really use it.
• Hear how the word and the sentences sound.

🎨 SEE WHAT YOU ALREADY KNOW
Tell LingoMingo your level once. Words you know turn green, and the words worth learning next turn orange, so you always know where to focus.

🧠 REMEMBER WHAT YOU LEARN
• Save words and whole lines with one click. Each word keeps the sentence and the video where you found it.
• Review with flashcards that come back just before you would forget them.
• Take your words with you: export them to other flashcard apps.

📺 FIND SOMETHING TO WATCH
• Browse the newest videos from hand-picked YouTube channels in 18 languages.
• See at a glance which videos have subtitles made by people, not machines.
• Pin your favorite channels to the top.

🌍 LANGUAGES
Arabic, Chinese, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Hebrew, Hindi, Italian, Korean, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish and Ukrainian.

🤖 POWERED BY THE AI YOU CHOOSE
Word meanings and example sentences come from AI. When a video has no subtitles in your language, AI translates them too. Connect the AI service you like with your own key (some have a free plan), or run a free AI model on your own computer. Your AI service may charge you for what you use.

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
• Your words and settings stay in your browser.
• The text you translate or listen to goes only to the AI service you choose.
